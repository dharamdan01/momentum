## 1. Getting Started — Your First Login

### Step 1: Open MOMentum

Go to the link shared by your team lead in your browser. This works on both computer and phone.

### Step 2: Register

- Click **"Register"**
- Enter your **name** (use the same name your team knows you by)
- Enter your **email**
- Choose a **password**
- Click **Register**

> **Already have a member profile?** If your name was added by someone else, just register with the same name. It will link to your existing profile.

### Step 3: Login

- Enter your **name** and **password**
- Click **Login**
- You'll stay logged in for **3 days**

### Step 4: You're In!

You'll see the **Dashboard** — your home screen. Everything starts here.

## 3. Recording a Meeting

This is the core of MOMentum — turning a meeting into documented, trackable work.

### Step 1: Create a Meeting

1. Go to the **Meetings** tab (click "Meetings" in the sidebar, or press `2`)
2. Click **"New Meeting"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — e.g. "Weekly Sync — Oct 14"
   - **Date** — when the meeting happened
   - **Participants** — who was there (comma-separated names)
4. Click **Create**

### Step 2: Add the Recording

You have **two options**:

**Option A — Upload Audio**
- Click **"Upload Audio"** on the meeting page
- Select your recording file (MP3, WAV, M4A, etc.)
- Most phone and laptop recordings work

**Option B — Paste Text**
- Click **"Paste Transcript"**
- Copy-paste the meeting notes or transcript
- Useful if someone already typed things up

### Step 3: Transcribe (if you uploaded audio)

- Click **"Transcribe"**
- The AI converts your audio to text
- Takes about **30 seconds per hour** of recording
- You can review the transcript after

### Step 4: Generate MOM

- Click **"Generate MOM"**
- The AI reads the transcript and creates a professional **Minutes of Meeting**
- The MOM includes:
  - Meeting details
  - Agenda items
  - Discussion summary
  - Action items with assignees and deadlines
  - Key decisions
- Takes about **10 seconds**

### Step 5: Extract Tasks

- Click **"Extract Tasks"**
- The AI pulls out every action item and creates tasks
- Each task gets:
  - A **title** (what needs to be done)
  - An **assignee** (who should do it)
  - A **deadline** (when it's due)
  - A **priority** (high / medium / low)
- Tasks appear immediately in the Tasks tab

> **That's it.** Five clicks — your meeting is fully documented with tracked tasks.

## 4. Managing Your Tasks

### Finding Your Tasks

Go to the **Tasks** tab (click "Tasks" in the sidebar, or press `3`).

### Two Views

**List View** — A searchable table of all tasks
- Use the **search bar** to find any task by name
- Use **filters** to narrow down:
  - **Status** — Pending, In Progress, Completed, or Overdue
  - **Assigned To** — Your tasks, or a specific team member's
  - **Priority** — High, Medium, Low

**Kanban Board** — Visual columns
- `Pending` → `In Progress` → `Completed` → `Overdue`
- Great for seeing the big picture at a glance

### Updating a Task

| What You Want to Do | How |
|:--------------------|:----|
| Start working on a task | Change status to **"In Progress"** |
| Mark a task as done | Change status to **"Completed"** |
| Change the deadline | Edit the deadline field |
| Change who it's assigned to | Edit the "Assigned To" field |
| Change priority | Click the priority badge and select a new level |

> When you complete a task, you'll get a **confetti celebration!** It's a small thing, but it makes closing tasks feel good.

### Creating a Task Manually

You don't need a meeting to create a task:
1. Go to the Tasks tab
2. Click **"New Task"**
3. Fill in the title, assignee, deadline, and priority
4. Click **Create**

## 5. Notifications & Reminders

MOMentum sends reminders automatically so you never miss a deadline.

### What You'll Receive

| When | What | Who Gets It |
|:-----|:-----|:------------|
| **3 days before a deadline** | "Your task [name] is due on [date]" | The person assigned to the task |
| **After a deadline passes** | "OVERDUE: [task name] — please take action" | The person assigned to the task |
| **Every 6 hours** | Team status summary (pending/done/overdue per person) | Management only |

### Setting Up Your Notifications

1. Click your **name** in the top-right corner of the screen
2. Click **"Notification Settings"**
3. Choose your preferred channel:

| Option | What Happens |
|:-------|:-------------|
| **Email** | Reminders go to your registered email address |
| **Telegram** | Reminders go to your Telegram chat |
| **Both** | You get reminders on email AND Telegram |

4. Click **"Save"**
5. Click **"Send Test"** to verify it works — you should receive a test message

### Setting Up Telegram Notifications

1. Open Telegram on your phone
2. Search for the MOMentum bot (your team lead will share the bot name)
3. Send `/start` to the bot
4. The bot replies with your **Chat ID** — it's a number like `123456789`
5. Go to MOMentum → Notification Settings
6. Paste your Chat ID in the **Telegram Chat ID** field
7. Select **"Telegram"** or **"Both"** as your preference
8. Click **Save**

### Setting Up Email Notifications

Email works automatically if:
- Your email address is in the system (check with your team lead)
- Your preference is set to **"Email"** or **"Both"**

> **Not getting emails?** Check your spam/junk folder. Gmail sometimes filters automated emails.

## 6. Managing Your Team (Management Only)

If your role is **management**, you have extra capabilities.

### What Management Can See

| Feature | Where |
|:--------|:------|
| **Everyone's tasks** | Dashboard → Team Workload section |
| **Team summary** | Automatic email/Telegram every 6 hours |
| **Overdue alerts** | Dashboard → Alerts section |
| **Member workload** | Dashboard → shows tasks per person |

### Management Actions

| Action | How |
|:-------|:----|
| **Send instant reminders** | Click the **"Send Reminders"** button in the top header |
| **Add a team member** | Team tab → Click "Add Member" → Enter name, email, role |
| **Edit a member** | Team tab → Click on a member → Edit their details |
| **Remove a member** | Team tab → Click delete on a member |
| **Trigger a summary** | Notifications → "Send Summary" sends the status report to all managers |

### Team Member Roles

| Role | Access Level |
|:-----|:------------|
| **management** | Full access — sees all tasks, gets team summaries, can manage members |
| **member** | Standard — can create meetings, manage their own tasks |
| **FDE** | Same as member |
| **consultant** | Same as member |

## 7. Dark Mode, Light Mode & Shortcuts

### Switching Themes

- Press **`T`** on your keyboard to toggle between dark and light mode
- The app also respects your system preference (if your computer is in dark mode, MOMentum starts in dark mode)

### Command Palette

Press **`Ctrl+K`** (or `Cmd+K` on Mac) to open the **Command Palette** — a search bar that lets you do anything:
- Type "new meeting" to create a meeting
- Type "tasks" to jump to the tasks page
- Type a member's name to find their tasks

### All Keyboard Shortcuts

| Press | What Happens |
|:-----:|:-------------|
| `Ctrl+K` | Open Command Palette |
| `T` | Toggle dark / light mode |
| `1` | Go to Dashboard |
| `2` | Go to Meetings |
| `3` | Go to Tasks |
| `4` | Go to Team |
| `M` | Create a new meeting |
| `N` | Create a new task |
| `?` | Show all shortcuts |
| `Esc` | Close any open dialog |

## 8. FAQ — Common Questions

<br>

**Can I use MOMentum on my phone?**

Yes. Open the same link in your phone's browser. Everything works — creating meetings, managing tasks, checking the dashboard. No app install needed.

<br>

**What audio formats can I upload?**

MP3, WAV, M4A, WEBM, MP4, OGG, and FLAC. Most phone recordings (voice memos) and laptop recordings work out of the box.

<br>

**How long does transcription take?**

About 30 seconds for a 1-hour recording. Short meetings are almost instant.

<br>

**What if the AI makes a mistake in the MOM?**

The MOM text is fully editable. Review it and make changes. Tasks can also be manually adjusted — change the assignee, deadline, or priority anytime. The AI gets it right about 90% of the time; you verify the rest.

<br>

**Can I create tasks without a meeting?**

Yes. Go to the Tasks tab and click "New Task". You can create standalone tasks that aren't linked to any meeting.

<br>

**Can multiple people create meetings?**

Yes. Any logged-in user can create meetings, upload audio, generate MOMs, and extract tasks. It's not limited to management.

<br>

**Can we track tasks across multiple meetings?**

Yes. All tasks from all meetings appear in one unified Tasks view. Filter by status, assignee, or priority regardless of which meeting they came from.

<br>

**I'm not getting email notifications. What do I do?**

1. Go to Notification Settings (click your name → Notification Settings)
2. Make sure your email is correct
3. Make sure your preference is set to "Email" or "Both"
4. Click "Send Test" to check
5. Check your spam/junk folder

<br>

**I'm not getting Telegram notifications. What do I do?**

1. Make sure you sent `/start` to the bot
2. Make sure your Chat ID is entered correctly
3. Make sure your preference is set to "Telegram" or "Both"
4. Click "Send Test" to check

<br>

**How do I change my password?**

Contact your team lead / admin to reset it. Self-service password reset is on the roadmap.

<br>

**Is my data safe?**

Yes.
- Passwords are encrypted (bcrypt) — even admins can't see them
- Login sessions use secure tokens (JWT) that expire after 3 days
- All data stays on your company's server
- Nothing is shared with third parties
- The AI APIs only receive meeting transcript text — no personal data, passwords, or files
